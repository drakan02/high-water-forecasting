## Method and metrics

The paper used SHOM's MAS (MArvé Simon) Species Concordance suite, selected Newlyn as its reference and reports one year of overlapping data. The public MAS implementation was not available here. The locked substitute is described in `reports/decisions/2026-11-09-species-newlyn-design-v1.md`: fit matching UTide coefficients to each station in 2006, estimate one complex transfer factor per finite UTide Doodson species code, apply the factors to Newlyn's predicted astronomical tide, and reconstruct the Avonmouth series in 2007. Species-unclassified constituents are listed and zeroed. A parallel direct Avonmouth UTide fit through 2006 is retained. This is a fixed band transfer approximation; it omits the MAS complex-envelope and detailed Eq. 2–4 procedure.

All errors below target **total observed Avonmouth H**, gauge-only; they are not surge-corrected and are not comparable to published Table 1 values. Both models use 8,759 identical hourly target timestamps and 704 identical eligible P3 high-water cycles (`099d208d9c934a1ae056c81e0eddc4db0f62bfa94e6265f296975fc7e58c2599`).

| Evaluation | Model | n | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hourly 2007 | UTide fit on Avonmouth 2006 | 8,759 | 43.86 cm | 53.90 cm |
| Hourly 2007 | Newlyn species-banded transfer fit on 2006 | 8,759 | 58.92 cm | 73.27 cm |
| P3 high-water cycles 2007 | UTide fit on Avonmouth 2006 | 704 | 39.38 cm | 49.30 cm |
| P3 high-water cycles 2007 | Newlyn species-banded transfer fit on 2006 | 704 | 26.48 cm | 33.90 cm |

Keep both results: the transferred model has worse hourly errors but lower paired peak MAE/RMSE than this one-year direct UTide baseline. The 2006-fit UTide values are not the stronger multi-decade P2 baseline and are included to compare two models with the same calibration year. The full comparison products and species factors remain in the ignored local output directory `data/clean/p10_species_newlyn/` with `run_manifest.json`, `monthly_source_coverage.csv`, hourly forecasts, peak forecasts, paired metrics and coefficient table. The manifest includes source and artifact hashes.
