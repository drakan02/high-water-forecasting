## Locked design and result

Design was frozen in `reports/decisions/2026-11-09-newport-network-design-v1.md` before fitting. UTide 0.4.0 OLS used 133,988 exact-hour QC-valid observations from 1993–2009, automatic constituent selection, Greenwich phase, mean/trend and nodal corrections. Hourly data 2008–2011 formed peak/lag context. There were 2,822 context cycles and 705 candidate 2010 cycles; 664 were QC-eligible.

The gauge-only ECM fit on 422 eligible 2009 peaks, used a 24-hour issue lead and immutable N−2/N−4/N−6 lag IDs. It forecast 621 cycles. Paired scoring used the same 605 cycles that were QC-eligible and forecastable for both methods. Shared cycle ID SHA-256: `f6507d5fabe9dc996305675050baba844139f5bb3f5b7b71692940b7e3a7cae2`. Target is total observed H; no surge was inferred.

| Model | n | MAE | RMSE | Bias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UTide fit 1993–2009 | 605 | 19.75 cm | 24.68 cm | +2.96 cm |
| ECM fit 2009 | 605 | 12.52 cm | 16.30 cm | −1.28 cm |

These gauge-only substitute metrics are not directly comparable with the paper's surge-corrected Table 2. The matrix `reports/results/model_port_period_target.csv` explicitly retains completed, not-run and blocked combinations. Network extent is two of the article's 44 stations. The ANN pilot in the paper is Avonmouth; the P10 Newlyn transfer is registered for Avonmouth, so this phase does not claim Newport ANN or SC scores.
