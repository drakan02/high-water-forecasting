## Delivered

- `dashboard/index.html` is self-contained, responsive and offline. It filters by port, evaluation period and model view; shows 2010 QC coverage, port MAE, high-water observations/forecasts, issue origin, N−2/N−4/N−6 lag detail, and the model × port × period × target table.
