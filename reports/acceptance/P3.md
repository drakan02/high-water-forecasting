## Decisions

The paper defines ECM on alternate high waters and notes that the harmonic and observed semi-diurnal maxima can occur at different times. SOP resolves the cycle interval as consecutive low-water minima of A(t); P3 uses hourly UTide minima and separate H/A maxima within left-closed, right-open cycles. This hourly sampling and interval convention is a group implementation choice, not claimed as the paper's hidden original peak-selection code. A gauge peak is eligible only when every hourly gauge sample in its cycle is QC-valid. Cycles outside the registered 8–16 hour duration bounds are flagged and made ineligible. No interpolation is used. Cycle IDs and sequence numbers are assigned before excluding a gauge-gap cycle. `H−A` is retained only as a gauge-only residual; `surge_m` stays null.

## Limits and conclusion

P3 is complete for the gauge-only path and supplies stable cycle IDs needed by P4. The selected maxima are hourly-sampled; GESLA release/channel/datum remain unresolved, and UTide is not demonstrated equivalent to EHM. There is no matched surge input, so P3 does not support Table 1b comparison. **Accepted for gauge-only P3.**
