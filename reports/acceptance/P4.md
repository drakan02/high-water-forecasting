## Locked adaptation

The paper's Eq. 5 uses `H−S−A` at alternate high waters. With no matching Avonmouth surge hindcast, this branch implements the explicitly permitted `gauge_only` variant: lag residual `e_N = H_N−A_N`, target correction `H_N−A_N`, and prediction of total observed `H_N`. It never substitutes `H−A` for surge and must not be compared directly with Table 1b. H and A maxima remain separate, as in the paper. The issue origin is the harmonic high-water time minus 24 hours. A lag is usable only if its observed H peak timestamp is at or before that origin; provider latency is unknown, so this timestamp availability assumption is recorded. The paper gives the alternate-high-water formula and separate peak timing in Eq. 5/6; the gauge-only target is a documented SOP branch choice.
