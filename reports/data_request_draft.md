# Draft data access request — Avonmouth pilot

This is a draft only; it has not been sent.

## To BODC

Subject: Avonmouth processed tide-gauge data for Hibbert et al. (2015) replication

Please provide the processed, quality-controlled Avonmouth tide-gauge series
for 1988–2007, especially calendar year 2007, from UK Tide Gauge Network series
303295. Please include available primary and secondary channels and the
original provider/BODC quality flags. The data are needed to fit the pilot
harmonic prediction over 1988–2007 and evaluate 2007 high-water peaks.

Please confirm the file format, column/header definitions, timestamp convention
(including GMT/UTC), level units and datum for each channel, QC flag meanings,
missing/sentinel values, duplicate/overlap behavior, access/reuse terms, and
whether annual files can be downloaded directly.

## To NTSLF / NOC

Subject: Avonmouth hourly CS3X residual-surge hindcast for 2007

Please provide the hourly residual-surge hindcast series used or suitable for
the Avonmouth 2007 pilot in Hibbert et al. (2015), for 2007-01-01 through
2007-12-31. The paper describes hourly CS3X surge hindcasts archived for model
validation. The Avonmouth gauge location is approximately 51.51089 N,
2.71497 W; please identify the model grid-cell centre used and the mapping from
the station location to that cell.

Please confirm product/model version, units and sign convention, timestamp
timezone and whether timestamps mark valid time, cadence/missing-value
conventions, any QC flags, whether this is residual surge versus total tide plus
surge, availability/issue-time metadata if retained, and data access/reuse
terms. If the exact paper input cannot be supplied, please identify the closest
licensed product and document its differences.
