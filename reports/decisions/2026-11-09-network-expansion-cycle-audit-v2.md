## Observation

The saved UTide reconstruction produced between 705 and 1,256 candidate 2010 intervals per port. The eligible interval count after the locked 8–16 hour duration rule and gauge QC is 13–701. Port Ellen has 938 candidate intervals (573 eligible), Bournemouth 1,256 (537 eligible), and Weymouth 1,242 (679 eligible). Sheerness has only 13 eligible intervals despite 706 candidates. These counts come from the saved peak tables, not a catalog claim.

## Decision

Keep the locked UTide fit, peak rule, all 44 paper ports, and every candidate cycle in the stored outputs. Do not tune interval thresholds after seeing model metrics and do not drop a port or poor-coverage peak. Keep the two article-specified ECM exclusions, Port Ellen and Bournemouth. Report per-port candidate/eligible counts and mark UTide/ECM results as gauge-only substitutes. The high candidate counts at Bournemouth and Weymouth are unresolved method/source behavior; they prevent claiming that each saved UTide interval corresponds one-to-one with a Hibbert high-water event. Treat the pooled metrics as comparisons over paired cycle IDs generated by this registered implementation, not as direct Table 2 reproduction.

A future method change needs its own pre-fit, versioned design decision and a fresh run. The direct Table 1(b) P8 conclusion is unchanged.
